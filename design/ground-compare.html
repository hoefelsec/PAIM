Decision · ground colour

Warm graphite vs. cool zinc

Same screen, same markup, same accent and semantics — the ground is the only variable. Both are rendered against a neutral #0A0A0A surround so neither gets a sympathetic backdrop.

The tradeoff: warm ground gives the cool blueprint accent a temperature contrast to work against, and lets the brass and clay project colours sit naturally. Cool ground is more familiar for a developer tool, but the accent and the ground share a temperature, so hierarchy has to come from value alone.

Flick between “Warm only” and “Cool only” — an A/B flicker in the same screen position reveals more than reading them stacked.

Watch the project tiles

Sidebar icons carry five different hues at 16–18% tint. On warm ground the clay and brass tiles look native; on cool ground they read slightly foreign, like they were pasted in.

Watch the brass row

The In Progress half-ring and the High priority bars are #C99A54 in both. On cool ground they gain contrast and pull the eye harder — which may be a feature or a distraction.

Watch the empty space

Most of this screen is background. Warm graphite has a faint paper cast in large fields; cool zinc reads more like a screen. That impression is the actual decision.
